# The first message

## Shape

1. **Open on them.** One specific thing you noticed about their company: the signal from lesson 3, a line from their site, a number from their own post. Not "I came across your profile".
2. **Say why it matters,** in one sentence, from their side.
3. **Ask one easy question.** An either/or they can answer in five words works best ("is intake handled at each location, or centrally?").
4. **Optional:** one line on what the product does, as a fact, not a pitch.

Under 90 words. No em dashes, no emojis, no exclamation marks. No "Hope you're well", "I'd love to", "quick call", "revolutionise", "game-changer".

## Before and after

Use a real account and a real signal from this course. The shape looks like this (Example Co and Examplefield Freight are made up, from a market unrelated to any course):

**The message everyone sends**
```
Hi Sam, hope you're well! I'm the founder of Example Co, an AI-powered platform that helps logistics teams streamline their dispatch workflows and unlock efficiency. Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call next week to explore how we could help?
```

**Yours**
```
Hi Sam, saw Examplefield Freight is hiring three dispatch coordinators this month.

Usually that means loads are coming in faster than the desk can book them.

Is dispatch run from each depot, or centrally from the main office?
```

**What changed:** opens on their hiring post, not on us. Names the pain in their words. One question they can answer from memory. No meeting ask in the first message.

## When the course has Otto connected

Otto can draft this message for each account and put it in a campaign for the founder to review. Nothing is sent without approval. Mention this in lesson 5 as the next step, in one line.

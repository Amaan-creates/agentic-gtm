# Buying signals

A buying signal is something you can see from outside that makes the prospect's pain urgent **now**. News is not a signal unless it changes what the buyer needs this quarter.

## Catalogue

Pick the 3 to 5 that fit this company's product. Each one needs a sentence on **why it means ready** for *this* product.

| Signal | What to watch | Why it often means "ready" | Freshness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hiring for the job the product does** | Job posts for the role the product helps or replaces (intake coordinator, SDR, data analyst) | The work is outgrowing the team. The budget for a hire can buy the product instead | 30 days from posting, or as long as the post is still live, whichever is later |
| **New leader in the buyer's seat** | A new VP or director in the persona's role (company announcement, their own profile) | New leaders review tools and processes in their first 90 days | 90 days |
| **Funding, acquisition or private equity deal** | Funding announcements, acquisition press releases | New money comes with growth targets. New owners standardise how things are done | 90 days |
| **Expansion** | New locations, new markets, a new product line | More volume through the same process | 60 days |
| **Public complaint about the pain** | Reviews or forum posts that mention the exact problem ("had to look through their faxes") | The pain is visible to their own customers | 90 days |
| **Tool or process change** | A job post that names a tool being adopted or dropped, a migration announcement | They are already changing how the work is done | 60 days |
| **Regulation or deadline** | A rule that takes effect for their industry | A date they can't move | Until the deadline |

## Finding a live example

For each signal you include, find **one real example** on an account from lesson 2:
- the company, the fact in one line, the date, and the source URL;
- search "<company> hiring <role>", "<company> appoints", "<company> acquires", "<company> new location";
- if nothing turns up for a signal on any account after a fair try, keep the signal but say "no live example this week". That is an honest and useful thing for a founder to see.

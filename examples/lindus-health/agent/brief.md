## What Lindus Therapeutics sells
Lindus Therapeutics (formerly Lindus Health, a tech-first CRO; the CRO business was sold to Curavit in August 2026) in-licenses clinical-stage drug assets, typically Phase 2-ready, in non-ultrarare chronic disease, and develops them through its own trial platform in the US, UK and Europe. Deal types: exclusive global licence, option, ex-APAC licence, co-development, SpinCo or joint venture. It has run 45+ trials. The "customer" it has to win is the licensor: the company that owns the drug.

## Ideal customer profile (the licensor)
- Industries: clinical-stage biotech and some pharma that own a drug with human data (Phase 1 complete, typically Phase 2-ready; Phase I-III ready accepted).
- Disease areas: cardiometabolic (obesity, diabetes, MASH), respiratory, psychiatry, dermatology, women's health, other non-rare chronic disease.
- Company size: small caps and venture-backed privates with about a year of cash or less, or that have pivoted away from the asset. Asian owners seeking a partner for rights outside Asia also fit.
- Regions: owner anywhere; the trial would run in the US, UK and Europe.
- The pain, in customers' words: "subject to receipt of additional financing"; "to be divested or partnered".
- Not a fit: acquired or being acquired by a larger company; cash-rich and running its own next trial with no partnering statement; rare/ultrarare disease or oncology focus; bankrupt or assets sold; no human data.

## Personas
- **CEO** (signs, with the board): owns the runway; cares whether a deal funds the next step without a dilutive raise.
- **Chief Business Officer / head of BD** (champion): runs partnering; cares about a credible partner that closes fast.
- **Chief Medical Officer** (evaluates): judges whether Lindus can run the next trial fast and clean.
- **Board chair / lead investor** (influences): wants value from the asset.

## Signals to watch
| Signal | What to look for | Why it means ready | Freshness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asset put up for sale or partnering | "Divest", "strategic alternatives", a CVR tied to one asset, or a pivot away from a disease area in a release or SEC filing | The owner has decided not to fund the asset | 90 days |
| Early readout, thin runway | A Phase 1b or Phase 2 topline release in the same quarter as a cash runway of about a year or less | Asset value just rose, next trial is expensive; best moment for a trial-running partner | 60 days |
| Next trial "subject to financing" | That phrase, or "pending additional capital", in a quarterly release or 10-Q | They cannot start without new money; a partner is the alternative to dilution | 90 days |
| New business development leader | Appointment of a Chief Business or Corporate Development Officer | New BD leaders build a partner list in their first 90 days | 90 days |
| Asian owner seeking a partner outside Asia | English or local business press about licensing rights outside China/Korea/Japan | This is Lindus's ex-APAC licence deal type | Until they sign |

Also report, as a warning rather than a signal, any account that was acquired, merged, went bankrupt or licensed the asset to someone else in the window.

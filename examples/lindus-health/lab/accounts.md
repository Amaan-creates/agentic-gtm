# 15 companies (shuffled)

1. **Upstream Bio** (upstreambio.com): Waltham, MA. Verekitug (anti-TSLP receptor antibody) positive Phase 2 in severe asthma and nasal polyps; $261.3M cash, runway through 2027; plans to start its own two Phase 3 trials in Q1 2027. No partnering plans announced.
2. **Corbus Pharmaceuticals** (corbuspharma.com): Norwood, MA. Oral CB1 inverse agonist CRB-913 showed 5% weight loss at 12 weeks in a Phase 1b obesity trial (Sep 2026); Phase 2 planned for H1 2027 while the company also starts a registrational oncology trial for a different drug.
3. **Mithra Pharmaceuticals** (mithra.com): Liège, Belgium. Women's health (estetrol for menopause). Filed for bankruptcy in 2024; its estetrol assets and subsidiaries were acquired by Gedeon Richter.
4. **Pila Pharma** (pilapharma.com): Malmö, Sweden. Oral TRPV1 antagonist XEN-D0501 for obesity and type 2 diabetes; two Phase 2a trials done; EMA approved a 12-week obesity trial in Aug 2026; the first part is funded, analysts assume more capital is needed.
5. **Reviva Pharmaceuticals** (revivapharma.com): Cupertino, CA. Brilaroxazine for schizophrenia, one Phase 3 completed; FDA wants a second Phase 3; enrollment pushed to H1 2027 "subject to receipt of additional financing"; moved from Nasdaq to OTCQB; says it is pursuing partnership opportunities.
6. **Terns Pharmaceuticals** (ternspharma.com): Foster City, CA. Had offered its metabolic assets (obesity, THR-beta) for partnering; Merck has agreed to acquire Terns for its leukemia drug.
7. **Skye Bioscience** (skyebioscience.com): San Diego, CA. Nimacimab (CB1 antibody) completed Phase 2a in obesity (extra weight loss on top of semaglutide); in Aug 2026 agreed to merge with Redx Pharma and refocus on fibrosis; nimacimab to be divested or partnered, with proceeds going to former Skye holders via a CVR.
8. **Palatin Technologies** (palatin.com): Cranbury, NJ. MC4R agonists for hypothalamic obesity, Prader-Willi syndrome and Bardet-Biedl syndrome (rare genetic obesities); first IND expected Q4 2026, no human data yet on the new molecules.
9. **Dermata Therapeutics** (dermatarx.com): San Diego, CA. Acne drug XYNGARI had positive Phase 3 (STAR-1) data, but the company withdrew the IND and pivoted to consumer skincare in 2025-2026; the drug program is paused.
10. **Neumora Therapeutics** (neumoratx.com): Watertown, MA. Lead depression drug failed Phase 3 and was discontinued (Jun 2026); cut 35% of staff; $147M cash expected to fund operations into Q3 2027 including its own planned Phase 2 of NMRA-511 (Alzheimer's agitation). No partnering plans announced.
11. **Vistagen** (vistagen.com): South San Francisco, CA. Fasedienol (social anxiety) missed a Phase 3 in Jun 2026; also has refisolone, a Phase 2-ready non-hormonal drug for menopausal hot flashes; cash into 2027; appointed a Chief Corporate Development Officer with licensing experience.
12. **D&D Pharmatech** (ddpharmatech.com): Seongnam, South Korea. GLP-1/glucagon agonist DD01 hit its Phase 2 endpoints in MASH (48-week biopsy data May 2026); China rights licensed to Salubris; CEO says it aims to sign a licensing deal for the rest of the world by year-end.
13. **Connect Biopharma** (connectbiopharma.com): San Diego, CA. Anti-IL-4R antibody rademikibart cut treatment failure by 66% vs placebo in a Phase 2 in acute asthma attacks (Sep 2026); $31.5M cash, about one year of runway; needs a Phase 3 next.
14. **Yarrow Bioscience, formerly VYNE Therapeutics** (yarrowbioscience.com): Bridgewater, NJ. After merging with Yarrow (thyroid autoimmune focus), the legacy dermatology BET inhibitors (VYN202, Phase 1b psoriasis signal, partial clinical hold being addressed) may be sold or licensed.
15. **Aclaris Therapeutics** (aclaristx.com): Wayne, PA. Immuno-inflammatory company with Phase 1b/2 antibodies in asthma and atopic dermatitis; cash to end of 2028; says it is pursuing strategic partnerships for its respiratory programs outside China.

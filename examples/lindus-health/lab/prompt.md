# Task: sort 15 companies for Lindus Therapeutics

Lindus Therapeutics (formerly Lindus Health) sold its CRO business in August 2026 and now in-licenses clinical-stage drug assets, then runs the trials itself. You are helping its business development team decide which companies are worth approaching as **licensors** (companies that could license, co-develop or sell an asset to Lindus).

## Ideal customer profile (the licensor)

- **Who:** a clinical-stage biotech or pharma company that owns a drug asset with human data (Phase 1 complete, typically Phase 2-ready; Phase I-III ready is acceptable).
- **Disease area:** non-ultrarare chronic disease in Lindus's areas: cardiometabolic (obesity, diabetes, MASH), respiratory, psychiatry, dermatology, women's health, or similar chronic conditions.
- **Why they would talk:** they cannot or will not fund the next trial alone: cash is short, the asset was deprioritised or shelved, the company pivoted, or they are openly seeking a partner (including Asian companies seeking a partner for rights outside Asia).
- **Regions:** assets developed or developable in the US, UK and Europe; the owner can be anywhere.

## Not a fit (disqualifiers)

1. Owned by, or being acquired by, a larger company (the decision is no longer theirs).
2. Funded through its own next trial and has said nothing about wanting a partner for the asset.
3. Focus is rare/ultrarare disease or oncology.
4. Closed, bankrupt, or its assets already sold to someone else.
5. No human clinical data on the asset.

## Instruction

For each company in the input, decide **Fit** or **No fit** using only the facts in its line and the rules above. Reply with one markdown table and nothing else:

| Company | Fit? | Reason |
|---|---|---|

Keep each reason to one line, and base it only on the facts given.

## What Tennr sells
Tennr automates referral intake for healthcare providers: it reads faxed orders, chases missing paperwork, checks eligibility and payer rules, and starts prior authorizations, so a small intake team can handle far more orders.

## Ideal customer profile
- Industries: home medical equipment (DME) suppliers in sleep (CPAP), respiratory (oxygen, ventilation, nebulizers) and diabetes (CGM).
- Company size: 2 to 40 locations, with a small intake team working hundreds of faxed orders a day.
- Regions: anywhere in the US.
- Ownership: independent, family or regionally owned.
- The pain, in customers' words: "I would have had to hire 3 to 4 more people to keep up with the volumes we were doing."
- Not a fit: owned by a national chain, a public roll-up, a hospital system or a private equity platform; single stores; cash retail; existing Tennr customers (NEB Medical, Home Medix, Eastern MedTech, Total Medical Supply, Performance Home Medical, iSleep, Kilgore's Respiratory, Medical Service Company, BetterNight, Total Sleep Management, King Drug, Ken Glover Drug).

## Personas
- **Owner, CEO or president** (signs): owns the P&L; cares because growth has meant hiring intake staff.
- **Director of intake or operations manager** (owns the problem, champion): owns the fax queue, backlog and turnaround.
- **Reimbursement or revenue cycle manager** (influences): owns denials and audit-ready paperwork.
- **Intake coordinator or branch CSR** (uses): keys orders in; high turnover.

## Signals to watch
| Signal | What to look for | Why it means ready | Fresh for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring for intake or authorizations | A job post for authorization specialists, intake, order entry or insurance verification, with a posting date | The paperwork is growing faster than the team; the budget for the hire can pay for Tennr | 30 days |
| A new branch | A new location on the company's site, or a new branch in Medicare's public supplier registry | More orders through the same intake team | 60 days |
| A change in the revenue cycle team | A job post or announcement for a reimbursement, billing or revenue cycle manager | A new manager reviews how documents flow in their first 90 days | 90 days |
| A rule with a date on it | A Medicare competitive bidding milestone (bid window, contract awards) for a product line the account sells, especially CGMs | Winning means tighter prices and more volume; losing means cutting cost. Both point at intake | Until the deadline |
| A new product line | The account starts offering CGMs, ventilation or sleep testing | New lines bring new document types and payer rules; one Tennr customer's CGM launch doubled its inbound documents | 60 days |

Before counting any signal, check the account is still independent (search "<company> acquired" and "<company> parent company"). If it has been bought by a chain, roll-up, hospital or private equity firm, report that instead of a signal.

## Message rules
- Open on the signal, not on us.
- One sentence on why it matters to them.
- One easy question, ideally either/or.
- Under 90 words. No em dashes, exclamation marks, "hope you're well", or meeting asks.
- Never name individuals you have not verified work there today.

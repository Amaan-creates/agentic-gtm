You are helping the founder of Tennr decide which companies to put on a target account list.

Tennr reads faxed referrals and orders, collects missing paperwork, checks eligibility and payer rules and starts prior authorizations, so a small intake team can handle far more orders.

## The ICP card

- **Who:** independent or regionally owned US home medical equipment (DME) suppliers.
- **Product lines:** sleep (CPAP), respiratory (oxygen, ventilation, nebulizers) and/or diabetes (CGM).
- **Size:** about 2 to 40 locations.
- **How work arrives:** referral-driven. Physician offices and hospitals send orders, mostly by fax, and the supplier bills insurance.
- **Not a fit:**
  - owned by a national chain, a public roll-up, a hospital system or a private equity platform (the decision is made at head office, not locally);
  - fewer than 2 or more than 40 locations;
  - retail-first stores that sell for cash and don't bill insurance;
  - existing Tennr customers: NEB Medical, Home Medix, Eastern MedTech, Total Medical Supply, Performance Home Medical, iSleep, Kilgore's Respiratory, Medical Service Company, BetterNight, Total Sleep Management, King Drug, Ken Glover Drug.

## The task

Sort the 15 accounts below into **fit** or **no fit**. Decide from the facts given, and apply every rule. A fact that sounds alarming but doesn't break a rule is not a reason to drop an account.

Answer with one markdown table and nothing else: `| Company | Fit? | Reason |`, one row per account, in the order given. "Fit?" is exactly `fit` or `no fit`. The reason is one line and names the rule or fact that decided it.

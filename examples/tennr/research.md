# Tennr

### What Tennr sells

- Hero: "Right patient. Right care setting. Every time." Tennr calls itself an agentic patient orchestration platform: it reads faxed referrals, collects missing documents, checks eligibility and payer rules, submits prior authorizations and routes patients.
- Who it says it serves (nav "Who we serve"): DME & Manufacturers, Infusion Providers, Fertility Clinics, Physician Groups, Hospitals & Health Systems, Specialty Pharmacies.
- DME page: pain lines "Inefficiency destroys margins", "Revenue growth shouldn't depend on headcount growth", "Every unconverted referral is permanent revenue loss". Categories: complex respiratory, sleep, urology, diabetes care, ostomy, wound care, mobility. Workflows: eligibility, prior auth, benefits verification, same-and-similar checks for Medicare, proof of continued need, refill rules.
- About: "Processed over 10 million patients in 2025"; office at 345 Hudson St, New York.
- No public pricing page (/pricing returns 404, checked 23 Sep 2026). Pricing is sales-led.

### Stage

- Series C, $101M at a $605M valuation, led by IVP, 18 June 2025; revenue "in the eight figures", triple the level at its Series B in October 2024.
- Series B $37M, October 2024 (listed on the press page).

## The ICP decision

- Considered: DME and sleep suppliers (11 of 21 stories), physician groups and specialty practices (5), infusion (1), home health, behavioral health and others (4), health systems (new hire, no story), pharma (new hire, no story).
- Picked: independent or regionally owned DME suppliers in sleep, respiratory and diabetes, 2 to 40 locations. Reason: most of the proof is there, the owner can sign, and the pain is quoted in their words.
- Not picked yet: health systems (6 to 12+ month cycles, seven-figure deals, per Tennr's own job post) and pharma (new Principal AE role, no public story). Judgement, not fact: we think a first-time seller learns faster on short cycles.
